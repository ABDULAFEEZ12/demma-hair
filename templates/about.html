Our Story

Welcome to DIMMAHAIRSHOP

DimmaHairShop has been in the hair business for over 9 years, serving customers who value quality, beauty and good customer service.

We are passionate about helping women find the right hair for their style, lifestyle and budget. Our collections include premium human hair wigs, bundles, closures, frontals and luxury donor hairs - carefully sourced for women who want beautiful, durable and luxurious hair.

We serve both retail and wholesale customers, including individuals, hair vendors, resellers, dropshippers, salons and bulk buyers.

Whether you are buying one wig for yourself or sourcing hair for your business, DimmaHairShop is here to make your shopping experience easy, convenient and reliable.
